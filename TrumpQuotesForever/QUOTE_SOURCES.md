# Quote sources and context

Checked October 28, 2026. Numbers follow `Quotes.lua`, then `MoreQuotes.lua`,
then `InsultQuotes.lua`, then `ArchiveQuotes.lua`, counting each string once. This is a curated collection,
not an exhaustive archive. Entries 121–200 are documented individually in
[INSULT_SOURCES.md](INSULT_SOURCES.md). Entries 201–1012 are documented in
[ARCHIVE_SOURCES.md](ARCHIVE_SOURCES.md).
These are excerpts of documented statements, not invented Trump-style lines.
Straight apostrophes and quotation marks replace typographic characters;
punctuation follows the cited transcript or reporting and is normalized where
needed. Lowercase openings and unfinished endings identify some partial excerpts.

The pool mixes false or unsupported claims, boasts, jokes, verbal slips, and odd
phrasing. A documented utterance is not evidence that the underlying claim is
true; nor does an incorrect statement by itself establish deliberate deception.
The addon attributes every message to Donald Trump.

## Evidence limits

The collection verifies that the quoted wording is documented in the cited
sources. It does not establish every anecdote as true or every boast as a
deliberate lie. The latest Husted entries use broadcast dates because the clips
do not establish a reliable original speech date here. Check each source for the
full question, surrounding remarks, and any editorial corrections.
