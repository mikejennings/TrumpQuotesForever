# Insult sources — entries 121–200

Checked October 28, 2026. Entries 121–180 are short excerpts; 181–200 are
standalone nickname fragments. They are documented statements or posts from
Trump's account. They are not independent findings about the people targeted.
Source wording and typos are preserved; apostrophes, quotation marks, and dashes
are normalized. Some excerpts begin or end mid-sentence.

The tweet records were checked against the
[Complete Trump Tweets Archive](https://github.com/MarkHershey/CompleteTrumpTweetsArchive).
Sixty-three entries additionally matched original records in the
[Trump Tweet Data Archive](https://github.com/bpb27/trump_tweet_data_archive).
The JSON ledger marks those cross-checks. Tweet dates below are UTC dates derived
from the original status IDs; the calendar date can differ in US time zones.
Some original Twitter links may require login or be unavailable; the archives
retain the records. The archive records include posts published by his account;
this does not establish who physically typed every post.

For a broader browsing index, see the
[list of nicknames used by Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_nicknames_used_by_Donald_Trump).
That index was used for discovery, rather than copied wholesale. The collection
here is curated and is not an exhaustive list of every insult.

## Heidi Cruz

Entry 159 is Trump's own threat concerning Heidi Cruz. His separate photo attack
was a repost of a comparison image, not an authenticated spoken quotation that
her appearance was ugly. The repost's caption is not loaded as Trump's original
words. [Contemporaneous Texas Tribune coverage](https://www.texastribune.org/2016/03/24/cruz-trump-leave-heidi-hell-alone/)
provides the image context. The suggestion that Cruz's campaign produced the
initial Melania ad was incorrect; it came from an independent group.

## Context for other excerpts

The John McCain excerpt comes from the July 18, 2015 exchange where Trump first
denied and then qualified McCain's war-hero status. The Carly Fiorina excerpt
was published in September 2015; Trump later claimed he meant her persona.
The three January 2024 entries concern Chris Christie, Ron DeSantis, and Nikki
Haley and come from one archived Truth Social post. The allegations concerning
fraud, dishonesty, investigations, intelligence, and personal conduct in the
remaining entries are Trump's rhetoric, not factual conclusions of this project.
