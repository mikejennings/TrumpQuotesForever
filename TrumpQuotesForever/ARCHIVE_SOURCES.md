# Archive sources — entries 201–1012

Checked October 28, 2026. This expansion adds 812 distinct excerpts
from original posts on Trump's account, dated 2011-10-20 through 2021-01-06.
These are attributed posts, not independent findings about people mentioned.
Inclusion does not establish that a claim is true, false, or deliberately deceptive,
or identify who physically typed a post. This is a curated selection, not all his insults.

Each entry uses one excerpt of at most 25 whitespace-delimited words per original
post. Reposts and posts containing quoted third-party passages were excluded.
Excerpts preserve wording and typos, with normalized typography and whitespace;
links at the end of a post may be omitted. Entries were reviewed to remove
promotional text, unclear fragments, duplicates, and similar repeated wording.

Records come from the [Complete Trump Tweets Archive](https://github.com/MarkHershey/CompleteTrumpTweetsArchive),
using its `data/realDonaldTrump_bf_office.csv` and `data/realDonaldTrump_in_office.csv`
files. Original API records in the [Trump Tweet Data Archive](https://github.com/bpb27/trump_tweet_data_archive)
were preferred when available. 560 excerpts match text in both archives.
`ARCHIVE_SOURCES.json` records the actual text-match result for each archive.
These are two archived copies, not two independently authenticated witnesses.
Original post links can require login or be unavailable; the archives retain records.
Dates are UTC, using the original API timestamp when available and the status ID
otherwise. Calendar dates can differ in US time zones.

Names/outlets listed below are mentioned in the excerpt; they are not necessarily
the insult's addressee. Some entries are political attacks or boasts rather than
personal insults. The original 200 entries retain their separate source ledgers.
